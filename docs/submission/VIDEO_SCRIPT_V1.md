Status: **LOCKED WORKING DRAFT**

This document records the currently approved story spine and narration direction for the OKX Dev Day submission video.

---

## SEQ-1 — Brand Hook
**Target: ~0:00–0:20**

Every small company has a mysterious employee called *somebody*.

Can somebody get quotes?

Can somebody chase this?

Can somebody figure out whether this can arrive by Thursday?

Usually, that somebody is you.

So we built **Somebody**.

---

## SEQ-2 — Every Company Makes or Buys
**Target: ~0:20–0:45**

Every company runs on the same decision, over and over again:

**Do we make this ourselves — or buy it from someone else?**

Build the software, or subscribe to it.

Hire the expertise, or outsource it.

Research it ourselves, or buy the data.

AI changes the economics of that decision.

Intelligence is becoming cheap to create.

But the things intelligence needs — proprietary data, specialist services, infrastructure, access — still have to come from somewhere.

So if we are building a company that can operate autonomously, it needs to do more than work.

It needs to know **what to make — and what to buy.**

---

## SEQ-3 — MAKE / BUY with OKX
**Target: ~0:45–1:15**

On the **MAKE** side, Somebody can build capacity inside the company.

It can create or reuse **Interns**, give them the tools they need, and let them do the work.

But not everything should be built internally.

Sometimes somebody else already has the data, infrastructure, expertise or service you need.

**That is where OKX AI comes in.**

OKX AI gives agents a marketplace where they can discover specialised services, hire other agents, and pay them for results.

And because the financial rails are built for agents too, the buying side does not have to kick back to a human checkout flow.

In Somebody, a BUY decision can become a real machine-to-machine transaction.

Somebody finds the external capability, evaluates the terms, stays inside its authority, and pays through the OKX stack.

**OKX AI provides the market.  
The agent payment layer makes the purchase executable.  
And X Layer provides the settlement rail.**

That closes the economic loop Somebody needs.

---

## SEQ-4 — Under the Hood
**Target: ~1:15–1:35**

Under the hood, Somebody runs as a management loop built with **LangGraph**.

It takes the founder’s goal, works out what needs to happen next, and sends internal work to **Interns** running on the **OpenAI Agents SDK**.

**Convex** keeps the company’s working memory — the objective, the evidence, the approvals, the purchases, and the results.

And whenever something meaningful changes, Somebody wakes up and looks at the situation again.

The model gets to reason.

But the application controls what is true, what the company is allowed to spend, and what actually counts as done.

**Decide. Act. Observe. Decide again.**

## SEQ-6 — The One-Person Company
**Target: ~2:25–2:50**

And this is where the idea gets much bigger.

As more capabilities become machine-buyable — data, infrastructure, specialist models, services, even access to people and the physical world — a company no longer needs to own everything it needs.

It just needs to know what belongs inside, and what should come from outside.

Somebody can build the internal capability.

Markets like **OKX AI** can supply the external capability.

And together, that starts to make the **one-person company** possible:

one person setting the direction, with the functional reach of a much larger company.

What to buy.

**The one-person company.**
