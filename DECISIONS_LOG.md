# Somebody × OKX — Product & Architecture Decisions

This file records the current decisions that define the product.

## Product identity

Somebody is the AI manager for the One Person Company.

The founder gives Somebody an objective. Somebody owns the work of defining success, assembling capability, choosing the next strategy, requesting authority when needed and staying accountable until the deliverable is verified.

## Generic objective engine

The product is built around Objectives, Outcome Contracts and Requirements rather than a fixed procurement or launch workflow.

The recorded launch scenario is one configuration of the engine, not the engine itself.

## Internal workers

Somebody can create or reuse persistent bounded workers.

Workers execute assignments. Somebody owns staffing, economic strategy and objective completion.

The founder-facing product name is **Intern**.

## MAKE / BUY

MAKE and BUY are requirement-level strategies.

A single objective may contain multiple internal and external actions.

Strategy is recomputed from current resources, evidence, capability, market options, economics and authority.

## External resources

The market is modeled as a resource market rather than an agent directory.

External services declare what resource and purpose they can fulfill, along with economic and execution facts.

## JEV

JEV is integrated into sourcing selection.

When multiple eligible options remain, JEV can select among the grounded alternatives. Eligibility and authorization remain deterministic application decisions.

## Founder economic authority

Paid acquisitions surface through Needs You.

Founder approval creates a bounded spend grant tied to the current objective and decision.

## OKX payment stack

Approved acquisitions execute through x402 and settle on **X Layer Testnet** for the recorded demo.

Payment state, settlement state and provider-result verification remain distinct so the system can resume and reconcile correctly.

## External results feed internal work

A verified purchase result becomes a company input.

Somebody can then resume internal work and update the final artifact using the acquired evidence.

## Final deliverable

The requirement graph resolves one terminal founder-facing deliverable.

That artifact is assessed against the objective's minimum completion bar. When revision is required, only the final deliverable is reopened for a bounded correction.

The verified artifact can be exported as PDF.

## Current model stack

GPT-family execution uses **GPT-6 Luna** through OpenRouter.

LangGraph coordinates the manager. The OpenAI Agents SDK runs bounded workers. Convex stores authoritative state.

## Demo framing

The recorded demo is based on a completed run configured to show both MAKE and BUY.

The scenario uses a simulated cross-platform research benchmark to make the two paths visible in one objective. The engine itself remains generic and selects strategies from current runtime facts.

Payments and settlement run on **OKX Testnet**.
