## Locked V6 direction

V6 preserves the approved V4/V5 information architecture and motion language while incorporating the approved Somebody + Intern duo asset system.

- left Objective rail;
- Somebody update card at the top;
- Activity as the main column;
- Deliverables + Checkpoints in the right rail;
- approved Intern character set and event-specific poses;
- transparent Somebody + Intern working duo on `/start`;
- short walking-duo transition after objective submission;
- coffee duo used for the calmer `Needs you` / waiting state;
- motion remains state-driven and sparse rather than decorative;
- Delegated activity layout keeps the V5.1 text-overflow fix;
- no production frontend/backend changes.

The approved duo asset bundle and usage guidance are committed under:
- `docs/design/prototypes/assets/somebody-intern-duo-assets-approved.zip`
- `docs/design/SOMEBODY_INTERN_DUO_ASSETS.md`

Both contextual-background and transparent-background variants are preserved in the bundle.

This checkpoint freezes the current design direction for production-integration planning. Further visual work should fix concrete defects or integration constraints rather than restart information architecture.
