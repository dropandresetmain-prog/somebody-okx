- `duo-working-transparent.webp`
- `duo-walking-bg.webp`

## Intended usage

- Working duo: /start hero / active work moments.
- Walking duo: brief "objective is moving" transition.
- Coffee duo: waiting / reassessment / softer review moments.

Both contextual-background and transparent-background variants are preserved.
The transparent variants are preferred inside the Somebody product shell so they inherit the warm paper surface rather than introducing a white-image rectangle.

The repository copies are UI-optimized WebP derivatives of the approved generated source assets.
