# Somebody × OKX — Product Experience

## Product feeling

Somebody should feel like a capable manager already running the company around the founder's objective.

The interface should communicate three things immediately:

- what the founder asked for;
- what Somebody is doing now;
- what changed because of the work.

The product is not presented as a chat transcript, workflow builder, agent marketplace or wallet console.

## Information architecture

### Left rail — Objectives

The left rail contains:

- Somebody identity;
- **Start a new objective**;
- in-progress objectives;
- objectives that need founder input;
- completed objectives.

### Main header — Objective

Shows the founder's objective and current high-level state.

### Somebody update

The primary update card answers:

> **What is Somebody doing now?**

It uses concise founder-facing language and the Somebody character.

### Activity

Activity is the main narrative of the work.

It shows consequential events such as:

- interpretation;
- delegation;
- research findings;
- MAKE / BUY decisions;
- founder approvals;
- external acquisitions;
- transaction progress;
- artifact changes;
- verification;
- completion.

### Right rail

The right rail keeps persistent context visible:

1. Deliverables
2. Checkpoints
3. OKX Stack

Needs You appears above these when founder authority is required.

## Start experience

Starting an objective is a dedicated product state.

The founder enters an outcome in natural language. The product then moves directly into the objective workspace where Somebody interprets and manages the work.

## Character system

### Somebody

Somebody is the accountable manager:

- calm;
- competent;
- mildly dry;
- visually compact and understated.

### Intern

Intern is the internal worker:

- eager;
- optimistic;
- energetic;
- clearly subordinate to Somebody's managerial role.

Character usage supports product state rather than decorating every screen.

## Activity grammar

Different business events use different visual treatments.

### Delegation

Shows Somebody assigning bounded work to an Intern.

### Evidence

Highlights a useful finding and its source.

### Decision

Shows meaningful alternatives such as MAKE and BUY, the selected path and concise rationale.

### External acquisition

Shows what Somebody acquired, the provider, economic amount and current transaction/result state.

### Founder approval

Uses a high-emphasis Needs You state with the exact action available.

### Artifact change

Shows how new evidence changed the founder-facing deliverable.

### Completion

Shows the verified deliverable and the final completion state.

## Motion

Motion is reserved for meaningful state changes:

- delegation;
- work beginning;
- findings arriving;
- decision selection;
- approval;
- transaction progression;
- artifact change;
- completion.
