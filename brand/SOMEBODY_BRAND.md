# Somebody Brand Guide

## Product

**Somebody**

**Tagline:** **Somebody has to do it. Now Somebody can.**

Somebody is the AI manager for the One Person Company.

The founder gives Somebody an objective. Somebody takes responsibility for moving it forward: organizing work, assembling internal capability, bringing in outside resources when needed, asking for founder authority at the right moment and returning with a verified result.

## Brand idea

The character embodies the feeling:

> I would rather not be cleaning this up, but obviously I will.

Somebody is calm, competent and quietly accountable.

## Character direction

Somebody is a small ambiguous non-human office creature.

Personality:

- mildly annoyed, never angry;
- extremely competent;
- dependable;
- calm under mess;
- understated;
- focused on getting the work finished.

Humour comes from the situation rather than sarcasm or incompetence.

## Visual language

Core traits:

- compact rounded body;
- warm off-white / pale beige tone;
- two small dark head tufts;
- half-lidded dark eyes;
- restrained mouth and brow movement;
- short rounded arms and feet;
- simple office props;
- soft premium 3D treatment;
- readable at icon, product and presentation scale.

## Product actions

Somebody can be shown:

- working on a laptop;
- reviewing information;
- delegating to Intern;
- comparing options;
- drinking coffee;
- following up;
- waiting;
- asking for approval;
- reviewing a transaction;
- presenting a final result;
- verifying completion.

## Expression range

Primary expressions:

- neutral;
- focused;
- mildly annoyed;
- concerned;
- satisfied;
- happy, used sparingly.

## Brand voice

Product copy is:

- calm;
- competent;
- concise;
- mildly dry;
- outcome-focused.

Useful language:

- Give Somebody an objective
- Somebody is on it
- Somebody found something
- Somebody needs your say
- Why Somebody chose this
- Needs your approval
- Done
- Done and verified

## Character balance

Somebody is the manager.

Intern is the eager internal worker.

The contrast gives the product warmth without turning the interface into a mascot show.

## Asset direction

Use the approved Somebody assets in `brand/assets/` as the visual reference for future poses.

New artwork should remain consistent with the current character silhouette, proportions, facial language and soft-rendered treatment.
